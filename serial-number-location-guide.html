Where to Find the Serial Number on Any Appliance

Major Kitchen Appliances

Label Location

Refrigerator

Common Spot:
Upper interior wall, fresh food compartment.

Check the interior sidewalls (left or right) or the ceiling. In some models, the sticker is behind the kickplate at the bottom or on the rear exterior panel.

Label Location

Range & Oven

Common Spot:
Inside the storage drawer at the bottom.

Look on the frame behind the door, or under the cooktop (lift-up style). Some double ovens have them on the inner side of the door frame.

Label Location

Dishwasher

Common Spot:
Inside door, left or right interior edge.

Open the door and inspect the top or side of the inner door panel. If not there, look at the base behind the kickplate.

Label Location

Cooktop

Common Spot:
Underside of the unit or side trim panel.

For drop-in and slide-in cooktops, tilt the unit or look on the underside near the edge. Some induction models place the label on the side trim or beneath a removable panel along the front lip.

Label Location

Microwave

Common Spot:
Door frame or back/side exterior panel.

Countertop models usually place the label on the back panel or one side exterior. Over-the-range units often use the door frame near the hinge. Built-in drawer microwaves keep the plate on the left interior wall of the cavity.

Laundry Appliances

Label Location

Washer

Common Spot:
Fixed inner rim of the door opening.

Open the door and inspect the inner rim of the door frame — not the moving door itself. Top-load washers typically place the label under the lid on the tub rim. Some Samsung and LG models put it on the rear panel.

Label Location

Dryer

Common Spot:
Inside door opening or door frame.

Check the door rim or the cabinet edge just behind the door opening. Some models place a secondary label inside the lint-trap slot. Older dryers and Whirlpool-family units may use the rear panel.

HVAC Systems

mode_fan

Central Air - Outdoor Unit

Labels on outdoor units face the elements and can fade. Use high-contrast lighting to read embossed text.

LOCATIONS:
  • Right-side panel near the refrigerant lines.
  • Access panel for the electrical connections.
  • Back panel near the coils (less common).
heat_pump

Furnace & Air Handler

Usually found behind the front access panel to protect the label from internal heat components.

LOCATIONS:
  • Inside the front door (service panel).
  • Inside side wall of the cabinet (requires door removal).
  • On the blower motor housing itself.

Plumbing & Water Heaters

Label Location

Tank Water Heater

Common Spot:
Upper tank body label, just below the top cap.

The label is typically on the front of the upper tank body, designed to face outward. It may wrap around the cylinder — check both the front and side. Gas units sometimes have a second label near the burner access area.

Label Location

Tankless Water Heater

Common Spot:
Front face label, sometimes inside front cover.

The label is usually visible on the front face without opening anything. Some brands place a second tag inside the removable front cover. The plate typically includes serial number, BTU rating, and flow rate.

Electronics & Devices

Label Location

Flat-Screen TV

Common Spot:
Back panel sticker or Settings → About.

Most flat-screen TVs place the serial label on the back panel near the lower corners. If the TV is wall-mounted, the serial number is also available in the settings menu under About or System Information.

Label Location

Laptop

Common Spot:
Bottom case sticker or battery compartment.

Flip the laptop over — the label is usually on the bottom case. Older removable-battery models sometimes hide the serial inside the battery compartment. MacBooks and Surface devices often have it laser-engraved on the case.

Label Location

Desktop Computer

Common Spot:
Back or side panel of the tower case.

Tower desktops usually place the serial on the back or side panel. All-in-one computers move the label to the rear of the display housing. The serial is also accessible through Windows System Information or macOS About This Mac.

Label Location

Gaming Console

Common Spot:
Back panel near the ports or system settings.

PlayStation and Xbox consoles place the label on the back near the power connection or port area. Nintendo Switch carries the serial on the back of the handheld unit. Most consoles also display the serial number in their settings menus.

Brand-Specific Nuances

Samsung
QR codes & barcodes on lower right-side exterior.
LG
High-gloss silver labels on side frames.
Carrier
Year is often encoded in the first 4 serial digits.
Kenmore
Model prefix (e.g. 110.) indicates actual maker.
Whirlpool
Uses large white labels inside doors or lid bases.
Apple
Laser-etched in tiny print on the bottom case.

Tips for Hard-to-Read Labels

The label is faded or dirty. What now?

expand_more
Try using a bright flashlight from a side angle to create shadows in the embossed text. You can also carefully rub a graphite pencil over embossed lettering to make it stand out. For dirty labels, use a damp cloth but avoid harsh cleaners that might dissolve the ink.

Can I find the serial number in the software?

expand_more
Yes, for modern "smart" appliances and electronics. Check the settings menu under "About," "System Information," or "Network Status." On many smart fridges, it's also located in the 'Info' section of the external touchscreen display.

Why do I need the serial number vs just the model?

expand_more
Manufacturers often change internal parts midway through a production year. The serial number identifies the specific "run" or date your unit was made, ensuring you order the exact replacement part that fits your specific build version.
