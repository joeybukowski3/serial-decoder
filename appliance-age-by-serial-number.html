Appliance age by serial number

How to find appliance age by serial number

Use this guide when you want to determine the age of a refrigerator, dishwasher, washer, dryer, oven, HVAC system, water heater, or other household equipment from the model and serial label.

Why the serial number matters

The serial number is usually the strongest clue for appliance age because manufacturers often encode the month, year, or production sequence there. The model number helps identify the product family, but the serial is usually better for age lookup.

Where to find the serial number on an appliance

Refrigerators often place the label inside the fresh-food compartment. Dishwashers usually place it on the inner door frame. Washers and dryers often place it around the opening or rear console. Ranges and ovens often place it on the oven frame. HVAC systems and water heaters usually use a rating plate on the cabinet or tank.

What to do after you find the label

If you have the serial number, go straight to the serial decoder or a brand-specific page like Whirlpool, Goodman, Samsung, or ASUS. If you only have the model number, switch to Smart Lookup.
