Our methodology
How do I find the age of my dishwasher?
Select your dishwasher's brand, enter the serial number exactly as shown on the label, and click Decode. The tool applies manufacturer serial format rules to return the manufacture year and month.
Where is the serial number on a dishwasher?
On most dishwashers, the serial number label is on the inner door edge — either at the top of the door or along the left side panel inside the door. Bosch places the label on the left inner door edge. GE and Whirlpool typically put it along the top inner door rail.
What if my dishwasher's serial number label is missing?
Use Smart Lookup. Enter the model number from the manual or any remaining label, plus the brand name. The AI will research the likely manufacture date range based on model release and discontinuation history.
How long do dishwashers typically last?
Most dishwashers have a typical lifespan of 9–12 years. Knowing the manufacture date helps you assess whether a repair cost is justified or whether replacement makes more sense given the unit's age.
