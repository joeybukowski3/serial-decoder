How to decode a GE serial number

Many GE-family appliances use the first character for month and the second character for year. That makes the beginning of the serial more important than the trailing sequence digits when you are checking age.

category

What the model number can tell you

The model number helps confirm the appliance family, approximate generation, and likely decade. That matters because GE year codes repeat, so a direct serial result may still need product-era context.

FAQ

How old is my GE appliance? expand_more

Use the serial number from the label. GE usually stores the age signal in the opening month and year letters rather than the model number.

Can the GE model number tell me the age? expand_more

Not reliably by itself. It is better for identifying the family and confirming the likely decade when the serial year letter repeats.

Why does the GE result still look estimated? expand_more

GE year letters repeat, so the decoder may still need model-family or installation-era context to confirm the decade.

Where is the GE serial number label located? expand_more

Most GE appliances place it on an interior frame, cabinet wall, or opening edge depending on product type.

Can this support claim documentation? expand_more

Yes. Just note when the decade is still estimated because the GE year code repeats across multiple cycles.
