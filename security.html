Transparency

Security & Data Notice

We explain how Decode My Item processes tool inputs, uses analytics, and protects information in transit.

What Data We Collect

Decode My Item is designed to minimize the information needed to provide a lookup. When you use the Serial Number Decoder or Smart Lookup:

  • Serial numbers and model numbers you enter are used solely to process your request.
  • Serial-decoder processing occurs in your browser where supported. Smart Lookup and AI Assistant requests may be processed by serverless APIs and third-party providers.
  • Please do not include personal, financial, account, or other sensitive information in lookup queries.
  • We do not sell user-entered serial numbers, model numbers, or lookup queries.

Smart Lookup & AI Processing

Smart Lookup uses AI to research model numbers and product descriptions. When you submit a Smart Lookup request:

  • Your input is sent to a third-party AI provider to perform research. Please do not include personally identifiable information in your lookup queries.
  • Responses may be cached or logged for operational, security, and reliability purposes. We do not promise that any provider retains no information.
  • Smart Lookup results are estimates and should not be used as sole documentation for legal or claims purposes.

Cookies & Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand traffic and tool usage. Google Analytics may process browser, device, page, referral, interaction, and IP-derived information and may use cookies or similar technologies, including a first-party analytics identifier. Hosting and API providers may also create normal server logs for security and reliability.

The site includes Google AdSense ownership-verification or readiness code, but it does not currently display approved ad units. Advertising disclosures and consent controls will be reviewed before visible advertising is activated.

Data Security

Connections use HTTPS, and we apply standard security practices intended to protect the site and its services. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Database Accuracy & Liability

Decode My Item's serial number database is hand-verified and maintained to the best of our ability. However, manufacturer formats can change without notice. Decode My Item makes no warranty as to the accuracy of decode results and is not liable for decisions made based on results returned by this tool. Always verify age determinations with supporting documentation when required for claims or legal purposes.
