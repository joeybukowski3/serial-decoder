How to decode a Maytag serial number

Modern Whirlpool-era Maytag products often follow a year-code-plus-week pattern similar to Whirlpool-family serials. Older Maytag products may instead use the last two characters for year and month, which is why the era selector can matter.

category

What the model number can tell you

The model number helps separate older legacy Maytag products from Whirlpool-era Maytag platforms. That context matters when the serial format repeats or when the decoder asks you to choose an era.

FAQ

How old is my Maytag appliance? expand_more

Use the serial number from the label. The current decoder supports both Whirlpool-era Maytag logic and older legacy logic when the era is known.

Can the Maytag model number tell me the age? expand_more

It is more useful for confirming the platform and era than for direct date decoding.

Why does the Maytag page ask for era? expand_more

Some Maytag serial layouts repeat or change by production era, so the correct path sometimes depends on whether the appliance is pre-2006 or post-2006.

Where is the Maytag serial number label located? expand_more

Maytag usually places it on the interior opening, cabinet wall, or product frame depending on category.

Can this support claim documentation? expand_more

Yes. It is useful for age support, but any era-based assumption should be documented when the serial family overlaps.
