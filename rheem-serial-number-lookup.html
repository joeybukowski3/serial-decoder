How to decode a Rheem serial number

Supported Rheem HVAC serial decoding commonly looks for a four-digit week/year block after an opening letter. In many supported cases, the first two digits of that block point to production week and the next two point to year.

What the model number can tell you

The Rheem model number helps confirm equipment family, tonnage, and generation. That supports replacement research and helps explain the context of an estimated week/year result.

FAQ

How old is my Rheem unit? expand_more

Use the serial number from the rating plate. The supported Rheem HVAC path commonly looks for a week/year block after the opening letter.

Can the Rheem model number tell me the age? expand_more

Not as directly as the serial number. It is better for identifying the family and helping with replacement research.

Why does my Rheem serial number not decode? expand_more

The serial may be partial, the label may be damaged, or the product may follow a different family than the supported HVAC path.

Where is the Rheem serial number plate located? expand_more

Most Rheem HVAC equipment places it on the outdoor cabinet or indoor access panel depending on product type.

Can this support claim documentation? expand_more

Yes. Serial-based age support is useful for HVAC claims, especially when the rating-plate photo is saved with the file.
