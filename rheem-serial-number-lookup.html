Choose Rheem HVAC or water heating before decoding

Rheem manufactures both heating-and-cooling equipment and water heaters, but the supported serial rules are category-specific. This page preselects HVAC and uses only the HVAC letter-plus-WWYY path.

For a tank, tankless, or hybrid water heater, use the water-heater age guide. Rheem's official water-heater documentation describes a ten-digit rating-label serial with its own month/year interpretation.

How to decode a Rheem serial number

Supported Rheem HVAC serial decoding commonly looks for a four-digit week/year block after an opening letter. In many supported cases, the first two digits of that block point to production week and the next two point to year.

What the model number can tell you

The HVAC model identifies component family and supports manual, capacity, and replacement research. This page does not infer tonnage or date from the model number.

FAQ

Is this page for Rheem water heaters? expand_more

No. It preselects Rheem HVAC. Use the water-heater guide for tank, tankless, and hybrid units.

What does WWYY mean on the supported HVAC path? expand_more

The two week digits come before the two year digits after the opening letter. X4502 therefore means week 45 of 2002.

Why does a valid Rheem water-heater serial fail here? expand_more

The categories use different decoders. A water-heater MMYY or embedded-WWYY format should not be sent through the HVAC rule.

Where is a Rheem split-system label? expand_more

Rheem identifies the back of the outdoor unit and the inside front cover of the indoor unit as model/serial locations.

Should indoor and outdoor components be researched separately? expand_more

Yes. Record the model and serial from each component rather than assuming the complete system shares one production date.

What if the HVAC serial is not letter plus WWYY? expand_more

Treat it as unsupported on this route and use official records or Smart Lookup. Do not rearrange characters to fit the example.
