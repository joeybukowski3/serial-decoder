Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Item Assist (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website at decodemyitem.com. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is processed, which third-party services receive it, and your rights regarding that data. By using Item Assist you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

a) Search Inputs

When you use the Serial Number Decoder, the serial number and brand you select are processed entirely within your browser. No serial number is transmitted to our servers during a successful rule-based decode. If the decoder cannot produce a result, a brief alert is sent to our internal monitoring system containing the brand name, serial number entered, product category, and a failure reason. This is used solely to improve decode accuracy.

When you use Smart Lookup, the free-text query you submit (maximum 200 characters) is sent to our server and forwarded to an external AI inference service to generate a response. See Section 3 for full details on how AI providers handle this data.

b) Contact and Feedback Forms

If you use our contact form or submit a decoder error report, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: your name (optional), email address (optional), and your message or feedback. This information is delivered to us via email and is not stored in any application database.

c) Automatically Collected Technical Data

Our hosting provider (Vercel) and advertising partner (Google AdSense) automatically collect standard technical data when you visit the site, including:

  • IP address (used for rate limiting; not linked to your identity)
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Device type and referring URL
  • Timestamps of page requests

Note: Item Assist does not use Google Analytics or any behavioral analytics platform. The only Google integration on this site is Google AdSense for advertising.

d) Cookies

  • Functional cookies: Small values stored in your browser (e.g., your last-selected product category) to improve your experience. These are not shared with third parties.
  • Advertising cookies: Google AdSense and its advertising partners may set cookies to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. These cookies may track activity across websites. You can opt out via Google’s Ads Settings (see Section 6).

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the Serial Number Decoder and Smart Lookup tools
  • To cache AI responses for up to 14 days to improve response speed and reduce redundant AI queries
  • To enforce rate limits and prevent automated abuse (using your IP address, which is not stored beyond the active rate-limit window)
  • To respond to contact form and feedback submissions
  • To serve contextually relevant advertising via Google AdSense

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not build individual user profiles. We do not use your data for any purpose beyond those listed above.

5. Security and Data Protection Notice

This section summarizes the technical and organizational measures in place to protect your data. It is intended for users, employers, and IT departments evaluating this service.

Encryption and Transmission Security

All connections to decodemyitem.com are encrypted using HTTPS. A Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS) header with a one-year maximum age, subdomain coverage, and preload status is enforced on every route, ensuring that browsers always connect over an encrypted channel. Data in transit to all third-party services (Gemini, Groq, Upstash, Resend) is also encrypted using TLS.

No Account or Personally Identifiable Information Required

Item Assist does not require user registration, login, or any form of identity verification. No account credentials, payment information, health data, or government-issued identifiers are ever collected. Serial numbers and model numbers submitted by users are not inherently personally identifiable information, though users should avoid submitting data they consider sensitive.

Secrets and Credential Management

All API keys and service credentials are stored exclusively as encrypted environment variables in Vercel’s infrastructure. No credentials are hardcoded in any source file or committed to version control. The project’s git history has been audited and confirmed free of exposed credentials or personal identifiers as of February 27, 2026.

Content Security Policy

A strict Content Security Policy (CSP) header is active on all pages. It explicitly allowlists only the domains required for the site to function (Google Ads infrastructure, Google Fonts) and blocks all other third-party scripts, frames, and connections by default. This reduces the risk of cross-site scripting (XSS) and unauthorized third-party data collection.

AI Model Training Commitment

This service uses the paid tier of the Google Gemini API. Under Google’s API Terms of Service, paid-tier customers’ prompts and responses are not used to train or improve Google’s models. The Groq API is bound by the same restriction under its Services Agreement. Neither AI provider we use will incorporate your queries into model training.

Minimum Data Principle

Each external service receives only the minimum data required for its specific function. AI providers receive only the query string — no IP address, user-agent, or session data is included in AI requests. Rate-limiting data (IP address) is handled separately by Upstash and is not shared with AI providers. Cached responses are stored by query text only, with no linkage to the originating user or session.
