Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Item Assist (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data. By using Item Assist you agree to these practices.

1. Data We Collect

a) Search Inputs

Serial numbers, model numbers, and free-text descriptions you submit to the Serial Number Decoder or Smart Lookup tool may be logged server-side for quality assurance, rate-limiting, and to improve decode accuracy. We do not link these inputs to personally identifiable information.

b) Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you visit Item Assist, our hosting provider (Vercel) and analytics/advertising partners may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Device type and screen resolution
  • Pages visited, referring URLs, and session duration
  • Timestamps of requests

c) Analytics Events

We may use Google Analytics to track aggregated usage patterns (page views, feature usage, error rates). This data is anonymized and used solely to improve the service. Google Analytics may set cookies as described below.

d) Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Functional cookies: Small values stored in your browser (e.g., your last-selected category) to improve your experience. These are not shared with third parties.
  • Analytics cookies: Set by Google Analytics to measure traffic and usage patterns.
  • Advertising cookies: If Google AdSense is active on this site, Google and its partners may set advertising cookies to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. These cookies may track activity across websites.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate, maintain, and improve Item Assist tools
  • To detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or excessive automated usage
  • To understand aggregate usage trends (no individual profiling)
  • To serve contextually relevant advertising via Google AdSense (if active)

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Item Assist uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel — hosting and serverless functions (processes IP addresses and request metadata)
  • Google Analytics — usage analytics
  • Google AdSense — advertising (may serve personalized ads based on interests)
  • Upstash Redis — rate-limiting storage (stores anonymized request counts, no personal data)
  • Resend — transactional email for user-submitted feedback (email address only if you contact us)

4. Data Retention

Server-side logs of search inputs are retained for a limited period for operational purposes. Retention by third-party providers varies by provider:

  • Google Analytics: Data retention is configurable; default is 14 months. See Google’s data retention documentation for details.
  • Google AdSense: Retention varies by provider; consult Google’s privacy policy for specifics.
  • Vercel: Log retention varies; see Vercel’s privacy policy.

5. Your Choices and Opt-Out

Cookies

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies. Disabling cookies may affect some site features (e.g., saved category preferences).

Google Ad Personalization

To opt out of personalized Google advertising, visit Google’s Ads Settings (search “Google Ads Settings” in your browser) and toggle off ad personalization. You can also install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on (search “Google Analytics opt-out”) to prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics.

Do Not Track

Some browsers send a “Do Not Track” signal. We respect browser privacy settings to the extent technically feasible, but third-party services (Google, etc.) have their own DNT policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

Item Assist is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of Item Assist after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

For questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use our Contact page.
