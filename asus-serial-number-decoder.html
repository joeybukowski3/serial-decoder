ASUS serial number decoder for manufacture date and device lookup

Use this page when you want to find an ASUS laptop serial number, motherboard serial number, or estimate manufacture date from the device label.

Where to find the ASUS serial number

ASUS laptops usually place the serial on the underside, on the original box, or in system information. ASUS motherboards may place the serial on the board itself or on a barcode label on the packaging. Desktops often place it on the side or rear of the case.

How to use the ASUS serial number decoder

Once you have the serial, move to the ASUS serial number decoder to estimate manufacture date. If you only know the model number, use Smart Lookup as a fallback for product-family research.

ASUS serial number decoder FAQ

How do I find the serial number on an ASUS laptop?

Check the bottom case, original packaging, BIOS, or system information. Older units may place it under the battery.

Can I estimate ASUS manufacture date by serial number?

Yes. Supported ASUS formats can be used to estimate manufacture year or date range.

Does this help with ASUS motherboards?

Yes. If you have the motherboard serial label, use the ASUS decoder. If not, use the model number with Smart Lookup.
