Serial Number Location Guide

Use this guide to find the model and serial number label on common appliances, HVAC equipment, and electronics before you run the decoder or call a technician. Start with the most likely spot for your item type, then work through the backup locations if the label is faded, hidden, or mounted behind a panel.

Once you have the serial number, go to the Decoder Tool.

How to Use This Guide

  • Select your item type and start with the common serial number location first.
  • Check the door frame, inner sidewalls, and access panels if the label is not in the first spot.
  • Use a flashlight and a side angle to read faded or embossed labels on outdoor or older equipment.

Kitchen Appliances

Refrigerator

Common Serial Number Location

  • Upper interior wall in the fresh food compartment.

Other Places to Check

  • Left or right interior sidewall.
  • Ceiling of the fresh food section.
  • Behind the bottom kickplate.
  • Rear exterior panel on some models.

Quick Tips

  • Start inside the refrigerator before pulling the unit away from the wall.
  • On smart fridges, the serial may also appear in the touchscreen info menu, but the physical label is still the best reference.

Oven / Range

Common Serial Number Location

  • Inside the bottom storage drawer.

Other Places to Check

  • Frame behind the oven door.
  • Under a lift-up cooktop on some ranges.
  • Inner side of the door frame on some double ovens.

Quick Tips

  • Check the drawer and door frame first before trying to move the appliance.
  • If the range has been cleaned recently, wipe around the frame edges where adhesive labels can blend into the enamel.

Dishwasher

Common Serial Number Location

  • Inside the door on the left or right interior edge.

Other Places to Check

  • Top edge of the inner door panel.
  • Side of the inner door opening.
  • Base behind the kickplate.

Quick Tips

  • Open the door fully and inspect both upper corners before removing any trim.
  • Use a flashlight on stainless interiors where the label can reflect and disappear at a straight-on angle.

Laundry Appliances

Washer

Common Serial Number Location

  • Door opening or lid frame.

Other Places to Check

  • Back of the control panel.
  • Rear cabinet panel.
  • Inside the detergent drawer housing on some front-load models.

Quick Tips

  • Top-load washers often hide the label under the lid rim rather than on the exterior body.
  • Front-load labels are usually easiest to see with the door fully open and room lights behind you.

Dryer

Common Serial Number Location

  • Door opening or lint screen housing.

Other Places to Check

  • Rear cabinet panel.
  • Back of the control panel.
  • Inner frame behind the dryer door.

Quick Tips

  • Check near the lint filter first because many labels are placed where service techs can find them fast.
  • If the label is dusty, wipe gently with a dry cloth before using water.

HVAC Systems

Central Air Outdoor Unit

Common Serial Number Location

  • Right-side panel near the refrigerant lines.

Other Places to Check

  • Electrical access panel.
  • Back panel near the condenser coils.
  • Inside the service panel on some packaged units.

Quick Tips

  • Labels on outdoor units face the elements and can fade. Use high-contrast lighting to read embossed text.
  • Take a photo and zoom in if the print is weathered or the label is mounted low to the ground.

Furnace / Air Handler

Common Serial Number Location

  • Inside the front access panel.

Other Places to Check

  • Inside wall of the cabinet after removing the service door.
  • Blower housing on some units.
  • Upper burner compartment label on some furnaces.

Quick Tips

  • Most indoor HVAC labels are placed behind the service panel to protect them from heat and dust.
  • Shut off power before opening panels if you are unsure about safe access.

Water Heaters

Tank Water Heater

Common Serial Number Location

  • Upper front of the tank on the rating plate.

Other Places to Check

  • Side of the tank near the gas control or thermostat.
  • Rear side of the jacket if the unit is installed in a tight closet.
  • Paper installation packet attached to the unit on some newer models.

Quick Tips

  • Look for a larger rating plate rather than a small sticker. Water heater labels usually include capacity and fuel type with the serial.
  • Use a side photo if the unit is tucked into a mechanical closet with limited access.

Tankless Water Heater

Common Serial Number Location

  • Side or lower front panel of the cabinet.

Other Places to Check

  • Inside the front cover near the wiring diagram.
  • Underside of the unit near the piping connections.
  • Installation card or service sticker left by the installer.

Quick Tips

  • Tankless units often place the label where the venting and electrical specs are easy for service techs to read.
  • If the front cover is removable, check for a duplicate label inside before searching around the wall mount.

Electronics

Television

Common Serial Number Location

  • Back panel near the input ports or power cord.

Other Places to Check

  • Behind the wall-mount bracket area.
  • Settings menu under About or Device Information.
  • Original box or purchase paperwork if the set is hard-mounted.

Quick Tips

  • Take care when pulling a wall-mounted TV away from the wall because labels are often centered near the rear connection panel.
  • Software menus can help confirm the number, but use the physical label for warranty or parts matching.

Laptop

Common Serial Number Location

  • Bottom case label or laser-etched print on the underside.

Other Places to Check

  • Battery bay on older removable-battery models.
  • BIOS or system information screen.
  • Original retail box or manufacturer support app.

Quick Tips

  • Apple laptops usually use very small laser-etched text on the bottom case.
  • Glossy silver labels can disappear under direct light, so tilt the device slightly while reading.

Game Console

Common Serial Number Location

  • Bottom or rear panel near the venting and power connection.

Other Places to Check

  • Settings menu under console information or system.
  • Original packaging for handheld or docked systems.
  • Sticker inside the battery compartment on some accessories and older handhelds.

Quick Tips

  • Consoles run warm, so labels may collect dust near the vents. Clean lightly before trying to read the print.
  • If the console is in a media cabinet, photograph the back label instead of pulling on connected cables.

Brand-Specific Label Clues

Samsung

Samsung appliance labels often include QR codes and barcodes, frequently on the lower right-side exterior or inner frame.

LG

LG commonly uses high-gloss silver labels on side frames, so you may need to tilt the door or lid to catch the print.

Carrier

Carrier HVAC labels are usually mounted on the exterior cabinet, and the year is often encoded in the first four serial digits.

Kenmore

Kenmore labels may include a model prefix such as 110. that points to the actual manufacturer behind the unit.

Whirlpool

Whirlpool often uses larger white labels inside doors, under lids, or along the cabinet opening where they are easy to inspect during service.

Apple

Apple serial numbers are commonly laser-etched in very small print on the bottom case of laptops and may also appear in system settings.

Tips for Hard-to-Read Labels

The label is faded or dirty. What now? expand_more
Try using a bright flashlight from a side angle to create shadows in the embossed text. You can also carefully rub a graphite pencil over embossed lettering to make it stand out. For dirty labels, use a damp cloth but avoid harsh cleaners that might dissolve the ink.
Can I find the serial number in the software? expand_more
Yes, for modern smart appliances and electronics. Check the settings menu under About, System Information, or Network Status. On many smart fridges, it is also located in the Info section of the external touchscreen display.
Why do I need the serial number instead of just the model? expand_more
Manufacturers often change internal parts during a production run. The serial number identifies the specific build or production period so you can match the correct replacement part, support bulletin, or warranty record.
