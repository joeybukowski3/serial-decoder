How to decode a Kenmore serial number

Kenmore decoding usually starts with the model prefix rather than the serial alone. Once the OEM platform is identified, the serial can follow Whirlpool-family, GE-family, LG-family, or other supported manufacturer logic.

What the model number can tell you

The first three digits of the Kenmore model number are often the key to identifying the actual manufacturer. That is why the model number matters more on Kenmore than it does on many direct-manufacturer brands.

FAQ

How old is my Kenmore appliance? expand_more

Use the model prefix to identify the OEM first, then decode the serial using the supported manufacturer path.

Can the Kenmore serial number tell me the age by itself? expand_more

Sometimes, but the model prefix is often needed first because Kenmore products were built by multiple OEM manufacturers.

Why does Kenmore need the model number more than other brands? expand_more

The model prefix often identifies who actually manufactured the appliance, and that decides which serial rule the decoder should apply.

Where is the Kenmore serial number label located? expand_more

Kenmore labels usually follow the same placement patterns as the underlying OEM product type: inside the cabinet, around the opening, or on the frame.

Can this support claim documentation? expand_more

Yes. Just note when the OEM is confirmed directly versus when the result still depends on a likely model-prefix match.
