Electronics Age Guide

How Old is My Electronics?

Find the right identifier first. Some TVs and computers expose supported serial date codes; others only support model-year or product-generation research.

Serial Number Location Guide → How Old is My Electronics?

Electronics & Devices

Label Location

Flat-Screen TV

Common Spot:
Back panel sticker or Settings → About.

Most flat-screen TVs place the serial label on the back panel near the lower corners. If the TV is wall-mounted, the serial number is also available in the settings menu under About or System Information.

Label Location

Laptop

Common Spot:
Bottom case sticker or battery compartment.

Flip the laptop over — the label is usually on the bottom case. Older removable-battery models sometimes hide the serial inside the battery compartment. MacBooks and Surface devices often have it laser-engraved on the case.

Label Location

Desktop Computer

Common Spot:
Back or side panel of the tower case.

Tower desktops usually place the serial on the back or side panel. All-in-one computers move the label to the rear of the display housing. The serial is also accessible through Windows System Information or macOS About This Mac.

Label Location

Gaming Console

Common Spot:
Back panel near the ports or system settings.

PlayStation and Xbox consoles place the label on the back near the power connection or port area. Nintendo Switch carries the serial on the back of the handheld unit. Most consoles also display the serial number in their settings menus.

Supported Electronics Research Paths

The result type differs by brand. Samsung TV and HP have supported serial-code paths; Sony and Vizio are model-based; Apple separates legacy serials from modern randomized identifiers; ASUS has a maintained opening-code decoder.

Samsung TV
Supported TV year/month character positions with repeating-cycle warnings.
HP
Supported year-digit and production-week fields, resolved with product context.
Apple
Legacy serial guidance plus an explicit stop for modern randomized identifiers.
Sony TV
Recent BRAVIA model-suffix years, not a serial manufacture-date claim.
Vizio TV
Model-era lookup because arbitrary Vizio serials are not date-decoded.
ASUS
Maintained opening-character year and month code path for supported serials.

Google Pixel and Panasonic identifier pages remain directly accessible for users who need label help, but they are temporarily excluded from the approval-facing index while their broad date rules are reviewed. Read the Methodology for how supported, estimated, and unresolved results are separated.

Serial Number, Model Number, or Smart Lookup?

Can every electronics serial number reveal a manufacture date?

expand_more
No. Some supported formats contain a year, month, or week code, while modern randomized identifiers and model-based TV systems do not expose an exact unit date. The brand guide should state which result type it supports.

When is the model number more useful than the serial?

expand_more
Use the model number for Sony and Vizio model-year research, to resolve a repeating HP or Samsung TV serial cycle, or to identify the generation of a modern Apple device whose serial is randomized.

What should I do when the identifier does not match a supported rule?

expand_more
Keep the result unresolved. Confirm the full label, identify the product family, and use Smart Lookup with the model and a short description. Do not borrow a rule from another brand or turn a release year into an exact manufacture date.
