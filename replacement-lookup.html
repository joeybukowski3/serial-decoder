Practical workflow

Replacement lookup for older appliances, TVs, HVAC, and general property items

When the original unit is discontinued or only partially identified, Decode My Item helps you move from vague description to a documented replacement path.

Best for model-based search

When to use this page

Use this workflow when you know the brand, model, family name, or a clear plain-language description of the item but do not yet have a reliable modern replacement candidate.

What Smart Lookup does well

  • Separates specific model searches from general brand-only searches.
  • Explains when a result is broad and prompts you to refine.
  • Surfaces a best-fit replacement and supporting confidence cues.
  • Works as a first-pass decision aid for claim, repair, and sourcing workflows.

Best search inputs

  • Brand + full model number
  • Brand + series or family
  • Brand + product type + capacity/size
  • General item description when model is unavailable
