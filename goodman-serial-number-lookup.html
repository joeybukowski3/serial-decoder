Goodman serial number lookup

Goodman serial number lookup for HVAC age and manufacture date

Use this page when you want to find out how old a Goodman AC unit, furnace, condenser, or air handler is from the model and serial plate.

Start with the Goodman data plate

Most Goodman serial number lookups start at the metal rating plate on the equipment cabinet. Outdoor condensers usually show it on the cabinet side. Furnaces and air handlers often place it inside the service panel area or on the cabinet door.

How to read a Goodman serial number

If you already have the full serial number, go to the Goodman serial number decoder. That is the fastest route for estimating HVAC age. If you only have the model number, use Smart Lookup to research likely age range and product family details.

Model number vs serial number for HVAC age

The serial number is usually the better path for age dating. The model number is still useful for narrowing equipment family, tonnage, and replacement context, especially if the serial tag is damaged.

Goodman serial number lookup FAQ

How do I tell how old a Goodman unit is?

Locate the rating plate, copy the serial number exactly, and decode it on the Goodman page. That is the best starting point for furnaces, condensers, and air handlers.

Where is the Goodman serial number located?

Look on the outdoor condenser cabinet, the furnace or air-handler panel, or near the service access area where the rating plate is mounted.

Can I use the Goodman model number instead?

Yes, but it is a fallback. Smart Lookup is the better route when only the model number is available.
