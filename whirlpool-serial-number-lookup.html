Whirlpool serial number lookup for appliance age and manufacture date

Use this page when you are trying to find the age of a Whirlpool refrigerator, dishwasher, washer, dryer, oven, microwave, or other appliance from the model and serial label.

How to do a Whirlpool serial number lookup

The fastest path is to locate the product tag and copy the full serial number exactly as shown. Once you have it, move to the Whirlpool serial number decoder to estimate manufacture date and age. If the label is damaged or you only have the model number, use Smart Lookup as your fallback.

Where to find the Whirlpool serial number

Whirlpool label placement depends on the product type. Refrigerators usually place the tag inside the fresh-food compartment. Dishwashers commonly place it on the inner door frame. Washers and dryers often place it around the opening or on the rear panel. Ovens and ranges often place it behind the door or on a rear data plate.

If you need a faster label guide by product type, use Find Model & Serial Number.

Model number vs serial number

The serial number is usually the better path for age lookup because manufacturers often encode date clues there. The model number identifies the product family, which can still be useful for research and replacement guidance. If you only have the model number, go to Smart Lookup.
