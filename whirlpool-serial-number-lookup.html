Why Whirlpool results can remain ambiguous

Whirlpool year letters repeat on a long cycle. The decoder therefore returns every supported candidate rather than treating a current-looking year as automatically correct.

A model family can narrow the cycle only when its documented era overlaps one candidate. Condition, styling, or an approximate installation date can be useful context, but they do not change the serial characters themselves.

How to decode a Whirlpool serial number

Most Whirlpool-family appliance serial numbers use a year code plus a production week. Nine-character serials commonly use character 2 for year and characters 3-4 for week, while ten-character serials commonly shift that year code to character 3 and the week digits to characters 4-5.

category

What the model number can tell you

The model number helps separate product families, confirm the era, and support the final decade choice when the Whirlpool year code repeats on a 30-year cycle. It is also useful when the serial label is incomplete or damaged.

FAQ

Why does Whirlpool serial length matter? expand_more

The supported nine-character format reads the year at character 2, while the ten-character format reads it at character 3. The production-week positions shift too.

Does Whirlpool encode a calendar month? expand_more

The supported path returns a production week, not a direct month. Converting that week to an exact calendar date would overstate the available precision.

Why can one Whirlpool letter represent two years? expand_more

The year code repeats on a long cycle. The decoder preserves both candidates until separate era evidence resolves them.

Can a model automatically choose the newest candidate? expand_more

No. A model narrows the cycle only when verified family evidence overlaps one candidate year.

Which characters does Whirlpool skip? expand_more

The current Whirlpool mapping skips I, N, O, Q, and V to reduce confusion. A serial containing an unsupported year code should not be forced into the sequence.

What happens when the week is 54 or 99? expand_more

The decoder rejects it. Supported production weeks must be between 01 and 53.
