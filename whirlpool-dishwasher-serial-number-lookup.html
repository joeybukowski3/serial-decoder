Whirlpool dishwasher serial number lookup

Whirlpool dishwasher serial number lookup for age and manufacture date

Use this page when you need to find the age of a Whirlpool dishwasher from the serial number on the inner door frame.

Where to find the Whirlpool dishwasher serial number

Open the dishwasher door and inspect the inner frame, side edge, or tub lip. In many Whirlpool dishwashers the model and serial tag is only visible with the door open.

What to do after you find the tag

If the serial number is readable, use the Whirlpool decoder. If the serial is damaged but the model number is still visible, switch to Smart Lookup.
