Help

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't have a serial number? ▾
That's what Smart Lookup was built for. Enter a model number, brand name, or even a plain-language description of the item and Smart Lookup will research the manufacture era and estimated age without needing a serial number at all.
How do I find appliance age by serial number? ▾
Select the brand, enter the serial number exactly as shown on the manufacturer label, and run the decoder. Item Assist applies known serial formatting rules to estimate the manufacture date and current age.
Where do I find the model and serial number on an appliance? ▾
Most appliances place the label on the door frame, inside the fresh-food compartment, behind a drawer, or on the rear/service panel. Our model and serial number location guide walks through refrigerators, dishwashers, laundry appliances, HVAC systems, TVs, and more.
Which brands are strongest on the site right now? ▾
Whirlpool, Goodman, Samsung, ASUS, GE, LG, Rheem, Carrier, and Trane are some of the strongest paths for serial decoding, age lookup, and model-based research.

ⓘ How to Use This Tool

Quick Decoding

  1. Choose the matching category button before selecting the brand.
  2. Enter the full serial number exactly as shown on the label.
  3. If the era selector appears, choose the correct manufacturing period.
  4. Run the decoder to reveal the date estimate and notes.

No Serial Number?

Use Smart Lookup above. It works with a model number, brand name, or a plain-language description when the serial tag is missing or unreadable.

Finding Serial Numbers

  • Refrigerators: Inside on the left or right wall
  • Ovens/Ranges: Inside the door or on the back panel
  • Dishwashers: Top edge of the door or side panel
  • Washers/Dryers: Inside the door or on the back
  • Water Heaters: On a sticker or plate on the side of the tank
Note: Many serial number codes repeat every 10-20 years. Always verify the decade using physical condition, model features, purchase receipts, or installation records.
