Help

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I don't have a serial number? ▾
That's what Smart Lookup was built for. Enter a model number, brand name, or a plain-language description and Smart Lookup will research the manufacture era and estimated age without needing a serial number at all.
How do I find appliance age by serial number? ▾
Select the brand, enter the serial number exactly as shown on the manufacturer label, choose the manufacture era if prompted, and click Decode. The tool applies known serial formatting rules to estimate the manufacture date and current age.
Where do I find the model and serial number on an appliance? ▾
Most appliances place the label on the door frame, inside the fresh-food compartment, behind a drawer, or on the rear or service panel. Our guide covers refrigerators, dishwashers, laundry appliances, HVAC systems, TVs, and more.
Which brands are strongest on the site right now? ▾
Whirlpool, Goodman, Samsung, ASUS, GE, LG, Rheem, Carrier, and Trane are some of the strongest paths for serial decoding, age lookup, and model-based research.
