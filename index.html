How to Use This Tool

For Quick Decoding:

  1. Select whether you're decoding an Appliance or Electronic item
  2. Choose the brand from the dropdown menu
  3. Enter the serial number exactly as shown on the product label
  4. Click Decode Serial Number to see the results

Finding Serial Numbers:

  • Refrigerators: Inside on the left or right wall
  • Ovens/Ranges: Inside the door or on the back panel
  • Dishwashers: Top edge of the door or side panel
  • Washers/Dryers: Inside the door or on the back
  • Water Heaters: On a sticker/plate on the side of the tank
  • TVs: Back panel or in settings menu

Important for Insurance Claims:

⚠️ Many serial number codes repeat every 10-20 years. Always verify the decade using:

  • Physical condition and wear of the appliance
  • Model number and feature comparison
  • Purchase receipts or installation records
  • Owner's manual print date

For claims processing: Document which method you used to determine the final manufacture date.

Brands Not Listed?

If a brand is not in our database:

  • Check if it's a rebrand (e.g., Kenmore uses Whirlpool, LG, or Frigidaire coding)
  • Look for the actual manufacturer on the model number label
  • Contact the manufacturer's customer service with the serial number
  • For water heaters, the manufacture date is often printed directly on the label

⚠️ For Insurance Claims: This tool provides estimates based on publicly available decoding information. Always verify results for claim accuracy. The difference in manufacture year can significantly impact claim settlements.
