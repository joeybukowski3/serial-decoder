Whirlpool refrigerator serial number lookup

Whirlpool refrigerator serial number lookup for age and manufacture date

Use this page when you need to find the age of a Whirlpool refrigerator from the serial number inside the fresh-food compartment.

Where to find the Whirlpool refrigerator serial number

Check the inside wall of the refrigerator compartment, behind the crisper drawers, or near the side wall by the door seal. Some units may also place a label near the rear service area.

What the serial number is used for

The refrigerator serial number is usually the best way to estimate manufacture date and age. If you have the serial, move to the Whirlpool decoder. If the tag is missing but you have the model number, use Smart Lookup.
