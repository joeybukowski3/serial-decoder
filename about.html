👥 Who We Are

Built for claims, appraisal, repair, and equipment-research workflows.

Our Story

Decode My Item was designed around real-world claims, appraisal, equipment-research, and repair workflows. The project addresses a common frustration: serial rules and label guidance are often scattered across manufacturer documents, service references, and product records.

We built what we wished had existed — a clean, fast, practical tool that consolidates decades of manufacturer serial number documentation into one place.

Decode My Item (decodemyitem.com) is provided by Item Assist. Decode My Item is the product and site name used for the decoder, its research rules, and its verification process.

Why It Matters

Well-supported manufacture-date estimates can contribute to fair, defensible claim research. The workflow is designed to assist with:

💵
Actual Cash Value
Calculating ACV based on verified age and expected useful life.
📋
Warranty Verification
Confirming whether a unit falls within manufacturer warranty periods.
⚖
Claim Documentation
Supporting defensible, well-documented age determinations in claim files.
⏱
Faster Settlements
Eliminating back-and-forth with manufacturers and tech support lines.

Our Database

Our decoder database is compiled from manufacturer technical documentation, service manuals, and trusted industry date-code references. It is reviewed and updated regularly to keep pace with evolving serial number formats across appliance and water heater brands.

Who This Is For

This tool is designed for insurance adjusters, independent appraisers, field technicians, contractors, and other claims professionals who need fast, reliable manufacture date information. It is freely accessible and provided as a professional resource to the industry.

For formal claim use: Results should always be corroborated with supporting documentation — installation records, purchase receipts, or physical inspection — when used in claim proceedings. Serial number formats can repeat across decades; always verify the correct era before finalizing a determination.
