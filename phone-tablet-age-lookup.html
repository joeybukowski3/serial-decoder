Where to find the serial number

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings > General > About > Serial Number — or on the SIM card tray
  • Samsung Galaxy: Settings > About Phone > Serial Number — or on the original box
  • Google Pixel: Settings > About Phone > Serial Number
  • All devices: Check the original retail box — the serial number is on the exterior label
  • iCloud: Apple devices can be looked up at appleid.apple.com under registered devices

Our methodology
How do I find the age of my phone or tablet?
Select the Electronics tab, choose your brand, enter the serial number from the device's Settings app or original box, and click Decode. For Apple devices, go to Settings > General > About. For Samsung, go to Settings > About Phone. The tool applies manufacturer serial format rules to return the manufacture year and month.
Where is the serial number on a phone or tablet?
For iPhone and iPad: Settings > General > About > Serial Number, or on the SIM card tray. For Samsung Galaxy: Settings > About Phone > Serial Number. For Google Pixel: Settings > About Phone. All devices also have the serial number printed on the original retail box. iCloud users can find it at appleid.apple.com under registered devices.
What if the decode doesn't match the release year?
Phones are often manufactured several months before they are released for sale. The manufacture date encoded in the serial number may be earlier than the release date you remember. This is normal — the decoder returns the actual factory manufacture date, not the retail launch date. For insurance purposes, the manufacture date is typically the more relevant figure.
How long do smartphones typically last?
Smartphones typically last 3–5 years in active use, though the hardware can function for 6–7 years or more. Apple provides iOS updates for approximately 5–7 years; Android support varies by manufacturer. The manufacture date is important for trade-in valuations, insurance replacement claims, and assessing remaining software support life.
